# Code of conduct

Cerberus is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions or anywhere the project is represented — is expected to make it a place where people want to contribute.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Be patient with questions, and specific and kind in reviews.
- Credit other people's work.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on what belongs in the project, and say so if you think it is wrong.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using the project's spaces to share exploits against systems the reporter is not authorized to test, or to ask for help attacking them.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks these rules privately to a maintainer of the repository on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban participants who break these rules, in proportion to what happened.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
